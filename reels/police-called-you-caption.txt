Police called you to the station? 4 things to know before you go:

1. Get it in writing. Police call you by a written order. Ask: am I a witness or an accused? (S.179 BNSS)
2. Some people can't be made to go. Women, men under 15 or over 60, persons with disabilities and the acutely ill are questioned at home. (S.179(1) BNSS)
3. You need not incriminate yourself. Answer truthfully, except questions that could expose you to a criminal charge. (S.180 BNSS, Art. 20(3))
4. You don't sign your statement. Police statements are not signed, and a confession to police can't be proved against you. (S.181 BNSS, S.23 BSA)

Before you leave home: tell your family where you're going and keep your lawyer's number with you.
