# सातासमुद्रापार — Shooting Script (3 episodes)

*A Marathi legal micro-drama · Adv. Akash Chikate, Pune*
*All characters and voices are AI-generated · dramatisation · general legal information, not legal advice*

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 3 episodes × ~60 s · 12 shots per episode · every shot in motion, every line spoken on camera with lip-sync (Kling 3.0 Pro, sound on, 5 s per shot).
**Look:** widescreen bars, teal-amber grade, film grain. Pune = night, sodium-orange, rain. Toronto = cold daylight, glass, CN Tower.
**No narrator.** The law is spoken by characters and shown on gold "⚖ कायद्याचा मुद्दा" (point of law) cards.

# भाग 2 — "परदेशी घटस्फोट: वैध की अवैध?"

**Point of law:** A foreign divorce decree is not automatically valid in India. It must be from a court of competent jurisdiction and on grounds recognised by the Hindu Marriage Act (*Y. Narasimha Rao v. Y. Venkata Lakshmi*, SC 1991; s.13 CPC). Remarrying on such a decree risks **bigamy**.
**Turn:** they choose mutual consent in Pune. **Cliffhanger:** "now wait 6 months?"

# भाग 3 — "६ महिने की ६ दिवस?"

**Point of law:** The 6-month cooling-off period can be waived (*Amardeep Singh v. Harveen Kaur*, SC 2017) when the couple has been separated for more than 18 months, reconciliation has failed and everything is settled. Consent can be withdrawn until the decree (*Sureshta Devi*, 1991), so it must be free and final.
**Resolution:** decree granted. *"वेगळं होणंही सन्मानाने होऊ शकतं."*
