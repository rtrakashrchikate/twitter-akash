FIR filed against you? Don't make these 5 mistakes.

1. Panicking without reading the FIR → Get a copy. You're entitled to one; most FIRs go online within 24 hours (Youth Bar Assn. v. UOI, SC 2016)
2. Ignoring a police notice → Respond and appear as directed (S.35(3) BNSS)
3. Calling or meeting the complainant → Stay away. Contact can look like pressure and hurt your bail.
4. Waiting to be arrested → Ask about anticipatory bail before arrest (S.482 BNSS)
5. Posting about the case online → Stay silent. Save chats, receipts and CCTV.

False FIR? The High Court has the power to quash it (S.528 BNSS).
