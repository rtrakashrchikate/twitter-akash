Police at your door at 2 AM? Don't panic. Know these 5 rights under the BNSS:

1. Ask why — police must tell you the grounds of arrest (S.47 BNSS, Art. 22(1))
2. Your family must be told — and where you are held (S.48 BNSS)
3. Meet your lawyer during interrogation (S.38 BNSS)
4. 24 hours — you must be produced before a Magistrate (S.58 BNSS, Art. 22(2))
5. Bailable offence? Police must tell you that you are entitled to bail (S.47(2) BNSS)

Save this. Send it to your family — someone may need it at 2 AM.
